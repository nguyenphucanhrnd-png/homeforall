# Video prompts (for the AI video tool) — one prompt per clip

Always paste the STYLE BLOCK first, then the CHARACTER block(s) that appear in the shot, then the SHOT text.
Attach the character images from `mascots/` as references if your tool supports it.

## STYLE BLOCK
```
Soft claymorphism stop-motion look, miniature diorama of a Ho Chi Minh City alley neighborhood.
Chunky rounded plasticine/clay shapes with subtle fingerprint texture, matte high-roughness material,
soft studio lighting, gentle shadows, shallow depth of field, smooth slow cinematic camera move in ONE direction,
no cuts inside the clip. 16:9. Palette: cream #FFF6EE, blue #3B8BD6, orange #F5822A, peach #FDD7B5,
deep navy #0B1F33 for night scenes. Hopeful, warm, playful but not childish.
STRICT: no text, no letters, no logos, no real brands, no watermarks, no photographs, no photorealistic animals,
no clear human faces (faceless clay figures only), no injuries, no gore, no distressing imagery. No audio needed.
```

## CHARACTER BLOCKS
```
MIU (cat): small orange tabby clay cat, soft darker orange stripes, white chest and white front paws,
pink nose, large amber eyes, small round ears. Same appearance in every clip.
DOG: golden-tan clay dog, floppy ears, dark nose, friendly open-mouth smile, cream chest patch. Same appearance in every clip.
```

## SHOTS

**s01-hero (6–8 s)** — `DOG` and `MIU` sit by the front door of a cosy clay house with an orange tiled roof, a heart-shaped window,
a wooden door, two round green clay trees and small flowers on a green base. Both animals breathe gently; the dog's tail wags softly.
Warm cream background with soft window light. Camera starts close on the door and slowly pulls back to show the whole house on its little diorama base.

**s02-alley-night (6 s)** — A narrow rainy alley at night in a clay Ho Chi Minh City neighborhood: corrugated tin awning, tangled wires,
a street lamp that flickers softly, wet pavement with small ripples, small potted plants. `MIU` huddles under the awning looking cold and small but not hurt.
Cold navy and blue tones with one warm lamp glow. Camera slowly pushes in at low angle.

**s03-rescue (6 s)** — Same alley, rain easing. A faceless clay volunteer figure (no facial features, simple rounded head) walks in carrying a pet carrier box.
A trail of softly glowing clay paw prints lights up on the pavement one by one toward `MIU`. The light shifts from cold blue to warm orange.
Camera tracks slowly along the paw prints at pavement height.

**s04-clinic (6 s)** — A bright blue clay veterinary clinic cutaway: exam table, cabinet with bottles, a wall shield with a cross.
`MIU` sits calmly on the table. Small clay badges (shield, medical cross, syringe, paw-print seal) pop up one by one and float gently around Miu.
Clean light blue palette. Camera orbits slowly 30 degrees.

**s05-foster (6 s)** — A cosy warm clay room, amber evening light, a round cushion bed with a paw-print pattern. `MIU` sleeps, chest rising and falling slowly.
A tiny clay plant and a window with soft light. Camera dollies in very slowly toward Miu's face.

**s06-connect-backdrop (5 s)** — A soft peach clay-textured abstract backdrop with small clay hearts and paw prints drifting slowly upward,
gentle light vignette. NOTHING ELSE: no pictures, no frames with photos, no animals, no text.
(Photo cards are added later by code.)

**s07-home-door (6–8 s)** — Evening. The wooden front door of a clay house opens inward releasing warm golden light.
Faceless clay family silhouettes (adult, adult, child) wait inside, softly lit. `MIU` walks up the stone steps and into the doorway.
Small lantern by the door, flower pots. Camera starts at the gate and slowly pushes forward, settling in front of the door.

**s08-network (6 s)** — Camera starts low in front of one glowing house and rises up and back to reveal a whole clay neighborhood at dusk,
dozens of small houses with lit windows. Thin glowing golden lines connect the houses one by one into a network. Navy sky fading to warm.

**s09-follow-up (5 s)** — Morning in the new home. `MIU` sits on a windowsill in warm light. On the wall, a clay calendar with three small hearts
(labelled only by position, no text) that light up one after another. A small clay houseplant sways slightly. Camera glides slowly sideways.

## Tips
- If a clip is almost right, regenerate only that clip; clips are independent.
- If the tool supports "image to video", use the last frame of the previous clip as the first frame of the next for continuity (optional: the page crossfades between scenes).
- Keep clips 5–8 s. Longer clips cost more bytes on the page.
